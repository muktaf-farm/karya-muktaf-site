It started as a hobby. Then it became the thing I love most.

Somewhere between my first shoot and my hundredth edit, filming stopped being something I did for fun and became my passion. I love the whole process: finding the angle, framing the shot, then sitting down in the edit to shape it.

I think in cinematic composition, so every frame is placed on purpose. And I colour grade every video myself, because the right colour makes a scone look warm enough to taste and a wedding feel like a memory worth keeping.

My goal is simple: make videos people remember, and want to share.

  • Cinematic compositionFraming and angles chosen to tell the story.
  • Professional colour gradingColour that matches the mood of your day or your brand.
  • Shot and edited by one personThe same eye from the first frame to the final cut.
